# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful disagreement, constructive feedback, and responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository maintainers' published contact channel. Maintainers will investigate promptly, fairly, and with appropriate confidentiality, and may remove contributions or temporarily or permanently exclude participants whose behavior is harmful.
